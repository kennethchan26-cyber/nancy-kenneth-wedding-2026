Getting there & parking

Church of St Benedict

MTR
Take the Tuen Ma Line to Sha Tin Wai Station. Leave by Exit C and walk to 7 Kong Pui Street.
Bus
Take KMB 86A or 89B to Sha Tin Wai Bus Terminus. The church is opposite the terminus.
On arrival
Guests are welcome from 1:30 PM. Please follow the directions at the church for seating.
Parking
Church parking is for registered vehicles only. If your vehicle is not registered, please arrange parking nearby or take public transport.

Regal Riverside Hotel

MTR
The hotel is about a five-minute walk from Exit C of Sha Tin Wai Station.
Bus
Take 284, 89X, 86K, 49X, 81C, 182 or A41 to the Regal Riverside Hotel stop on Tai Chung Kiu Road. Stops for opposite directions are on different sides of the road; check the stop for your route.
On arrival
Follow the signs to the Bauhinia Room on 3/F. Guests are welcome from 6:00 PM.
Parking
Parking is available on B1. For availability and rates, please call the hotel: 2649 7878.

From the church to the hotel

If you are travelling from the church to the hotel, use the walking directions below. The hotel welcomes guests from 6:00 PM.
