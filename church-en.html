Church of St Benedict

The church where we will celebrate our Nuptial Mass

← Back to wedding details

The Church of St Benedict is in Sha Tin Wai, where we will celebrate our Nuptial Mass. From the entrance to the altar, its design reflects the life of faith.

From entrance to altar

01

The cross and “Peace”

A cross stands at the highest point of the building. Above the centre of the foyer, the Chinese characters for “Peace” remind parishioners to carry the peace of the risen Christ into daily life.

02

A skylit foyer

A skylight in the foyer roof brings daylight into the building and gives the approach to the church a quieter atmosphere.

03

The baptismal font

Beyond the doors, the font stands centrally and points towards the altar. The parish explains this journey as new life through baptism leading towards the Eucharist.

04

A golden path overhead

As you walk towards the altar, a golden feature runs along the ceiling. The parish describes the route as a “path of light”.

05

Light of the Holy Spirit

The tall stained-glass windows on the right of the church symbolise the light of the Holy Spirit’s descent in the parish’s account of the design.

06

The round altar

The round altar represents unity and communion, expressing the parish’s sense of being one family.

A brief history

  1. 1987St Benedict Parish was established. Its first Mass was celebrated that September at Immaculate Heart of Mary College in Jat Min Chuen.
  2. 1990The groundbreaking and blessing for the present church building took place.
  3. 1993The five-storey church was completed and consecrated on 20 February.

The church is named for its patron, St Benedict, whose motto is described by the Diocese as “Prayer and Work”.

Architecture: Church of St Benedict parish, “Church Design”; history and photo: Catholic Diocese of Hong Kong, Catholic Heritage. This is a short guide for wedding guests.
