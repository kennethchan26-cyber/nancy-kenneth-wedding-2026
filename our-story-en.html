Nancy & Kenneth

Our Story

28 November 2026

← Back to wedding details

Our story began with a cat.

Kenneth and Nancy met on a dating app. One loved nature; the other had a profile photo holding a glass of red wine. Their styles seemed worlds apart. Yet Nancy was the first to press “Like”. What caught her eye was not the wine, but Bagel, Kenneth’s British Shorthair cat, in the photo. A fellow cat lover, she fell for him at first sight. That was how our unexpected story began: Bagel had become our little matchmaker.

Walking together in faith

Nancy was baptised in 2013; Kenneth had already enrolled in the catechumenate before they met. A trip together to the Trappist monastery on Lantau Island touched Kenneth deeply. He was baptised in 2025, and we have supported one another and grown together in faith ever since.

A quiet moment of certainty

Once, Kenneth visited the Assumption Cathedral in Bangkok on his own. In the deep stillness of the church, he knew with certainty: “Nancy is the one for me.” Later, he brought Nancy back to the cathedral. Someone happened to be practising the pipe organ, and that sacred, moving moment became an inspiration for the music at our Nuptial Mass.

Two cat lovers, one home

Nancy is a respiratory nurse who works day and night shifts, while Kenneth commutes to Cyberport each day. We understand how demanding each other’s work can be and promise to be one another’s best listener and safe haven. With Kenneth’s Bagel, Coco cared for by his mother, and Nancy’s cat family—鵪鶉, 肥妹 and 波波茶—our home is sure to be full of little paw prints.
